# Heating and boiler

Boiler: Vaillant ecoTEC plus, serviced every October by ThermoFix (contact: 600 555 210).

- Normal pressure is between 1.0 and 1.5 bar. Below 0.8 bar, open the filling valve under the boiler slowly until it reaches 1.2 bar.
- Error F.22 means low water pressure. Error F.28 means the gas did not light: press reset once; if it happens again, call ThermoFix.
- The thermostat programme: 21 °C from 7:00 to 9:00 and from 18:00 to 23:00 on weekdays, 20 °C the rest of the day, 17 °C at night.
- Bleed the radiators in November, starting with the one in the bedroom.
