# Expenses policy (summary)

- Train or plane in economy class for trips under 6 hours.
- Hotel up to 140 EUR per night in big cities and 100 EUR elsewhere.
- Meals: up to 45 EUR per day when travelling; keep every receipt.
- Claims go through the portal before the 5th of the following month. Late claims are paid a month later.
- Home office: up to 300 EUR a year for a chair, a desk or a monitor, with the invoice.
