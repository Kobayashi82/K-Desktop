K-Desktop

K-Desktop turns any key, mouse button or combination into a sequence of actions: open programs, arrange windows, change the volume, take screenshots, call Home Assistant, type text, read text off the screen… The same commands can be launched from a menu at the mouse, a search box, your voice, your phone, the network, or automatically when something happens on the PC.

On top of that, the optional assistant module puts characters on your desktop that you can talk to. They think with a local AI model, carry out your commands with gestures you can see, and come with tools for files, the web, the screen, music, timers, notes and your own documents.

Warning

K-Desktop 5 is used every day but is still under active development: things change from one version to the next.

In a few words

  • Commands: actions bound to shortcuts (CTRL + ALT + O, Mouse 5, CTRL + Wheel Up…), globally or for one program.
  • QuickMenu and launcher: a menu at the mouse and a search box that find and run everything.
  • Events: «when this happens, run these commands» (a time, a program opening, the PC idle, a USB drive…).
  • Macros and K-Board: record keyboard and mouse, remap keys and buttons per program.
  • Voice: your own wake word, recognition on your PC (no internet), dictation.
  • Phone: a web app and an Android app with your commands, a touchpad, a keyboard and the assistants' chat.
  • Five languages: English, Spanish, French, German and Italian.
  • Private: speech, voices and the AI run on your PC. See Privacy.
