Layouts

A layout cuts your monitors into zones and says which programs go in each one. Then it puts them like that whenever you want: with a hotkey, from a QuickMenu or on its own, with an event. A layout covers every monitor; one without zones in it is not touched.

  1. Place your programs the way you like (the browser on the left, the editor on the right, music on the other monitor…).
  2. Layouts → New layout: it already has the windows open now, each one as a zone with its program. Give it a name («Work»).
  3. Test puts it now. Create a command that applies it leaves you a command ready to give a hotkey.

Save windows replaces the zones of every monitor with the windows open at that moment: each monitor is cut by its windows, and each zone gets the programs of its windows (a maximized one, the whole screen; minimized ones do not count). Zones never overlap: windows one on top of another share a zone, and a wide free area becomes an empty zone. Each program also remembers the exact place and size of its window, so putting the layout leaves everything as it was.

Zones

At the top of Zones there is a pill per monitor: the connected ones and, faded, the ones that were connected at some point (K-Desktop remembers them), so you can prepare the TV layout even when it is not plugged in. A dot on the pill means that monitor has zones in this layout. Below is the drawing of the chosen monitor:

  • Click a zone to mark it (a click outside the zones unmarks it); another click on the marked one cuts it in two where the dotted line shows (vertically; with Shift, horizontally).
  • Drag the lines between zones to change their size (they stay at a half, a third or a quarter when you pass close).
  • The ✕ on a line joins its two zones, with their programs.
  • Templates: whole screen, 2 or 3 columns, grid, one big and two small, or 2 rows. The programs each zone had go to the new zone where its middle fell. Remove zones leaves the monitor without zones.

Under the drawing are the programs of the marked zone, as in K-Board's Only in these applications: type the program or pick it from the list and press Add, or press the cursor button and click its window. A zone without programs is only there to snap windows into.

Programs saved with Save windows have a pin: they go to the exact place their window had, shown dashed in the marked zone. Right-click → Fill the zone forgets that place, and the program stretches to its zone, like the ones you add by hand.

When it is put

  • If the program is open, its window goes to its zone (or to its saved place, if it has a pin); it does not open another one. A whole-screen zone leaves it maximized. With several windows of the same program (two of Chrome), it tells them apart by their title, else by order.
  • If it is not open, it is skipped, unless Options → Open the programs that are not open is on: then it opens it and places it as soon as its window appears. Microsoft Store apps cannot be opened this way. The notice says which ones were not open.
  • Monitors are recognized by their name, even if Windows numbers them differently. If a zone's monitor is not connected, its programs go to the main one, in the same place.

Using it

  • The action Window layout puts a layout from any command.
  • With a Screen plugged in or out event it is applied on its own: for example, a «Movie» layout when the TV is plugged in and a «Work» one when it is unplugged.

Snapping windows into the zones

Each monitor uses the zones of the layout put on it last (remembered after a restart). A monitor where none was ever put does not snap.

  • Shift + drag: while you drag a window by its title bar, hold Shift and the zones of each monitor show on top; the one under the mouse is marked and, when you drop it, the window fits into it (a whole-screen zone maximizes it). If you let Shift go, the zones disappear and the window stays where you drop it; Esc cancels. It is turned off in Options → Snap with Shift while dragging (it applies to all layouts).
  • Next or previous zone: the Window action has To the next zone and To the previous zone. They follow the zones' numbers and, after the last one of a monitor, go to the next monitor. Options → Create the zone hotkeys leaves you both commands made with Win + Alt + → and Win + Alt + ←.
  • Snapping always fills the zone, even if the program has a saved place.
