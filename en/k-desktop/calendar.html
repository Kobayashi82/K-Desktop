Calendar and agenda

K-Desktop keeps your appointments in one place: calendars of its own, your Google calendars and any .ics address (Outlook, holidays, sports…). It reminds you before each event, fires events, has actions to create events and join meetings, and the assistants can check and change your agenda.

The Agenda

Agenda, in the side menu above History, is there while some calendar has Show in the agenda. It shows the events, each in its calendar's colour, in two ways:

  • List: the days from today on, with today always at the top (even with nothing on it). The event going on now has Now, and the ones already over are dimmed.
  • Month: the whole month; all-day events are a band in their colour and the rest show their time. The arrows (or the mouse wheel) change the month, +N more opens that day in the list and a double click on a day adds an event on it.

Today goes back to today in both. The coloured buttons at the top show or hide each calendar (at least one has to be shown). Also:

  • New event, or a click on one to Edit it (or View it, if its calendar is read-only); right click: Join, Copy and Delete.
  • Join opens the Teams, Meet or Zoom link of an online meeting.
  • Search events…, Show earlier days and Show more days.
  • Update now reads the server calendars again; Import .ics and Export to .ics move events between calendars and other programs.

An event has a title, calendar, start and end (or All day), Repeats (daily, every weekday, weekly, monthly on a day or on «the second Tuesday», yearly, every so many of them; for ever, until a day or a number of times), Reminder, location, Meeting link (found by itself in the location or the notes) and notes. Changing or deleting a repeating event asks: This one only, This and following or All of them. An event from a read-only calendar can only be viewed.

Calendars

Settings → Calendar lists every calendar. Each one has its name (the assistant knows them by it: «put it in Work»), colour, Show in the agenda and a Reminder for new events. Default calendar is where new events go when you do not say another one.

  • New calendar: your own (Work, Family…), kept on this PC and in its backups.
  • Subscribe to an .ics: a read-only calendar from an address, read every so often (15 minutes by default). It is the way to see Outlook, or Google without connecting the account (Where to find the .ics address explains how). The address is kept encrypted: a secret calendar address lets anybody who has it read the calendar.
  • Google: connect the account in Settings → Accounts → Google (calendar only, or read and edit), then Show its calendars and choose which ones. Google asks for your own free application (a Client ID); How to get the Client ID walks you through it. K-Desktop never sees your password: you sign in in the browser.

Reminders

Settings → Calendar → Reminders chooses How it reminds you: an on-screen message (OSD), a notification (with Join when the event has a meeting link) or an assistant says it (if it is on the desktop; otherwise an OSD). Google reminders here too is off by default, because the phone already gives them. Try a reminder shows one.

Actions and events
Create event (action) Adds an event at a time or in a while, with title, location and notes that can have variables
Join the meeting (action) Opens the link of the current meeting (from 10 minutes before it starts) or the next one
Calendar event (event) Some minutes before an event, when it starts or when it ends: from a calendar, with a word in the title, online meetings only, with a location… The commands get {appointment}, {appointment_time}, {appointment_place}, {appointment_notes}, {appointment_link} and {appointment_calendar}

10 minutes before a Work meeting: do not disturb and open Teams.

The assistants

With the Assistant module, the tools Check the calendar, Add an event, Change an event and Delete an event: «what do I have tomorrow?», «put the dentist on Friday at 5», «move the meeting to 11», «cancel Thursday's dinner». Adding never asks, changing asks until you say «don't ask again», and deleting always asks; all three can be undone.
