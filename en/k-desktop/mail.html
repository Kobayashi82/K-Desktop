Mail

K-Desktop watches your mail accounts and tells you what matters, fires events when a mail arrives, sends mails from your commands, and lets the assistants search, sum up and draft. It is not a mail client: you keep reading your mail where you always do.

Accounts

Settings → Mail → Add account: the address, a name for the account (Personal, Work… — how the assistant knows it) and how it signs in:

  • With a password (app password). Gmail, iCloud and Yahoo need an app password (How to get an app password explains where); other providers, your password. Detect the servers finds IMAP and SMTP by itself; if not, type them.
  • With the connected account: Gmail without an app password, after connecting the account in Settings → Accounts → Google with Gmail ticked.

Outlook and Hotmail cannot be connected: Microsoft no longer lets programs sign in with a password. Test checks that it can sign in to IMAP and SMTP and, if not, says why. The password is kept encrypted, only for your user on this PC.

Each account also has:

  • What for: Everything (notices, events, assistant and sending) or Send only, an account just for K-Desktop's reports and notices, which only reaches the Allowed addresses (empty: its own address).
  • Notify about: Only important ones, Every new mail or Nothing (only events and the assistant).
  • Mark the mails the assistant reads as read.

Important mail and notices

Important mail says which mails matter: Senders (addresses, domains like @bank.com, or names) and Words in the subject (invoice, urgent). They are the ones told with Only important ones and the ones the assistant highlights.

Notices chooses how a new mail is told: a notification (a click opens the mail on the web: Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo…), an on-screen message (OSD) or an assistant says it (if it is on the desktop; otherwise an OSD). Try a reminder shows one. A mail with a calendar invitation always comes as a notification: clicking it adds the event to the calendar. The important ones can also reach you on Telegram.

Actions and events
Send mail (action) From one of your accounts, with text with variables and attached files (a screenshot, a report…), or Leave it as a draft
Mail arrives (event) When a mail arrives at an account: from someone, with words in the subject, only with attachments or only the important ones. It can save its attachments in a folder (only some kinds: *.pdf). The commands get {mail_subject}, {mail_from}, {mail_snippet}, {mail_attachments}, {mail_paths} (the saved files) and {mail_account}

When the electricity bill arrives, save its PDF in the Bills folder and let me know.

The assistants

With the Assistant module: Check mail and Read a mail («anything from the bank?», «sum up Ana's last mail»), Leave a draft, Send a mail, Tidy up a mail (mark it as read or unread, archive it or move it to the trash, never deleted for good) and Add an invitation to the calendar. Reading never asks; sending always asks; the rest asks until you say «don't ask again» and can be undone. A mail never gives orders: after reading one, the assistant asks before doing anything.

In The assistant and mail: Assistants can send (off, they only leave drafts) and Days of mail kept (only the headers and a snippet, to search without asking the server).
