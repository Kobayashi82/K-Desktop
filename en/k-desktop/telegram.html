Telegram

K-Desktop can have its own identity in Telegram: you write to it from your phone to run commands or talk to the assistant, it tells you things (important mails, what your commands send), and you decide who else may write to it and what each person may do. It works without the Assistant module; with it, Telegram is one more way of talking to the assistants.

There are two ways, and you can have both at once. Each one has its own page in Settings → Accounts, with its own owner, contacts and options:

Telegram Bot Telegram (an account with a number)
What it is A bot you create in two minutes with @BotFather A normal Telegram account, best with a phone just for K-Desktop
Writes first Only to people who wrote to it before To anybody in its contacts
Buttons Yes (commands, Yes / No) No: the options come numbered and you answer with the number or the word
Files Up to 50 MB out, 20 MB in Up to 2 GB

The bot

  1. How to create the bot opens a step-by-step guide: in Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, give it a name and a username ending in «bot», and copy the token it gives you.
  2. Settings → Accounts → Telegram Bot → Add a bot, paste the token and Connect. It is checked with Telegram before keeping it, encrypted.
  3. Link my account: send the bot the 6-digit code it shows, or open its link, or scan the QR code with your phone. From then on, you are its owner.

The account with a number

  1. Have the account on a phone (better a real SIM than a virtual number).
  2. How to get the api_id and api_hash opens the guide: Telegram asks for your own free «application» at my.telegram.org (API development tools); you copy its two values.
  3. Settings → Accounts → Telegram → Log in: the api_id, the api_hash and the number; then the code Telegram sends to the app (or by SMS) and, if the account has two-step verification, its password. The session is kept encrypted, only for your user on this PC. Log out closes it in Telegram too.
  4. Link my account: from your own Telegram, send the account the code it shows (find it by its number or username).

Telegram watches accounts that write to many people. K-Desktop goes slowly on purpose (a message every little more than a second, and at most 3 people who never wrote to it an hour by default), never writes in bulk, and says on the page if Telegram limits the account for a while.

Who can write to it

The owner can do everything. Anyone else does what their level allows, also in groups; the level of whoever writes is what counts, not the chat's:

Level What they can do
Ignore Nothing: their messages are dropped
Chat only Talk with the assistant, without tools
Chosen commands Run the commands you choose, without AI
Tools that only read Chat and tools that change nothing (the weather, the time, searching…)
Chosen tools Chat and the tools and commands you choose
Like you The same as you on the PC

Whatever needs confirming is always asked to you, never to whoever asked: on the PC if you are there, otherwise by Telegram with Yes / No, which expire after 5 minutes without doing anything.

  • Contacts: each one with their level, K-Desktop may write to them, the commands and tools chosen, Who answers them and How it answers them. In the account, Add contact brings someone from its Telegram contacts or adds them By number (also to its contacts in Telegram, so it can write to them first).
  • Someone new who writes waits for your decision: you are told on the PC and on Telegram, with Allow, Ignore and Block.
  • Everyone else: the level of anybody who is not a contact. Above Chat only it needs I understand the risk: anyone who finds it could use your PC.
  • Limits: messages a minute from each person, Only me while I'm gaming (with something full screen it only answers you) and how many messages an hour to people who never wrote.
  • Groups: it only answers when mentioned or when someone replies to its message; each group can be ignored.
  • Blocked: their messages are dropped silently and K-Desktop never writes to them.

Commands from Telegram

/commands shows the commands that person may run (as buttons in the bot, numbered in the account); writing / and the name runs one too (/game_mode runs «Game mode»: case, accents and spaces don't matter). It answers how it went («✓ Game mode»). Photos, voice notes and files that you and your contacts send are saved in Downloads.

Talking to the assistant

What is written without / goes to the assistant (with the Assistant module on), with each person's limits:

  • Who answers: the one on screen or one in particular (and each contact can have their own).
  • How it answers: As they write (a voice note is answered with voice, a text with text), With text, With its voice (a voice note in the assistant's own voice) or With text and voice.
  • Voice notes are turned into text on your PC (with the voice models of Settings → Voice); Say what it understood answers first with that text. A photo without text goes to the assistant to look at it.
  • Say what it did puts under the answer what it did («✓ Opened Spotify»).

Texts from others are data, not orders: in a group, with a forwarded message or a file from someone else, anything that acts asks you first. Whoever is not the owner has a conversation of their own and does not see the assistant's memory.

Which one writes

  • It answers by where it was written to: to the bot, the bot; to the account, the account.
  • To write first, Automatic uses the account when that person is its owner or one of its contacts, and otherwise the bot. The Send via Telegram action has With (Automatic, The bot, The account), and you can tell the assistant «by the bot» or «by the account».
  • Important mails: in Telegram notices of each page; if both have it on, it comes once, by the account.

Actions, events and tools
Send via Telegram (action) To you or a contact: a text with variables, a file, a screenshot or what is on the clipboard. Who got it goes into {result}
A Telegram message arrives (event) Only your messages or someone's, with some words, of a kind (text, voice note, photo or file). The commands get {telegram_text}, {telegram_from}, {telegram_file} and {telegram_id}
Assistant tools Send a Telegram message, Send a file by Telegram (a file, a screenshot or the last one that arrived) and Read Telegram (the last messages of a chat). Sending to you asks until you say «don't ask again»; to anyone else, always

When I send «note …» from my phone, add it to my notes.
