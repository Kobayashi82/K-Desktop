PublishPanel(JsonObject)

Publishes the data its panel.json binds to (Panel → Assistant; also the images of a gallery with «source» in its settings). It is merged with what it published before: objects key by key, anything else (numbers, texts, lists) replaces. Publish everything in Enter(EntranceKind); after that, what changed is enough. Cheap to call: the host sends it at most four times a second.

SetExtraState(string, ExtraState)

Shows, greys out or hides one of its extras in its right-click menu and its panel buttons, by what makes sense right now («Repeat the race» only after a race). Every extra is Shown each time it is loaded. Commands and voice still reach OnExtraAsync(string, CancellationToken): say there why it cannot be done. Cheap: only changes are sent.

SetVoiceMood(string?)

The situation its voice speaks in: error (it crashed), happy (it celebrates) or any other its voice profile names in its moods; null, as usual. The effects of that mood are added to what it says from then on. Wherever it is, it sounds as if it were on the monitor in use.
