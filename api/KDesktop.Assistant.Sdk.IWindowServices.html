TryMove(long, PixelRect)

Moves (and resizes) a window at once, for frame-by-frame dragging (its visible part: no shadows nor invisible borders). A window of an administrator's process is out of this process's reach: K-Desktop moves it, a little behind, at most about 30 times a second. False when it cannot be moved (it is gone, or maximized).
