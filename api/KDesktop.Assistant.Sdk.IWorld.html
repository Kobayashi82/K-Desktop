ListenToMusic(bool)

Listens to the rhythm of what the speakers play, to dance on the beat: Music is only measured while some assistant listens. It reads the output's peak meter (the bar of Windows' volume mixer: nothing is recorded), a hundred times a second while something sounds and a few times while it is quiet.
